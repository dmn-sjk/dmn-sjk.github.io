Hi! I am a PhD candidate at Poznan University of Technology, affiliated with the Institute of Robotics and Machine Intelligence, and in collaboration with IDEAS NCBR, where I am a member of the Continual Machine Learning Group.

My current research focuses on deep learning, machine perception, and robotics, with an emphasis on test-time adaptation. My research focuses on developing robust, intelligent perception systems capable of continuous adaptation in dynamic environments. Drawing on a hardware-aware mindset from my background in embedded R&D for bionic prostheses at Aether Biomedical, I am also highly interested in optimizing inference efficiency. I am the Principal Investigator (PI) for a research project on efficient real-world TTA, supported by a competitive grant from the Polish National Science Centre. Currently, as a MARS Fellow at the Cambridge AI Safety Hub, I benchmark LLM capabilities for agentic AI development. In general, I am interested in all things related to machine learning and robotics.

Please feel free to reach out if you're interested in my research or would like to join me for climbing or running.
